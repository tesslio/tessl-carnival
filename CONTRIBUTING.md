# Contributing

The carnival is a starter repo for a workshop. The way to build on it is in your
own fork: fork it, set up a factory, and let your factory grow your carnival.
[`WORKSHOP.md`](WORKSHOP.md) walks you through it.

## Issues and pull requests

Only collaborators can open issues and pull requests here. That keeps this
repository as the clean starting point that everyone forks.

In your fork, you can open as many as you like. The starter issues show up
under **Issues → New issue** once you've asked your agent to **Set up my fork**.

## Questions, ideas and bugs

- **Show what you built** in
  [Show your carnival](https://github.com/tesslio/tessl-carnival/discussions/13).
- **Ask a question, share an idea, or tell us something's broken** in
  [Discussions](https://github.com/tesslio/tessl-carnival/discussions), or on
  the [Tessl Discord](https://discord.com/invite/jbb2vHnHZQ).
- **Found a security problem?** Don't post it in public. See
  [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

Everyone here follows the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
