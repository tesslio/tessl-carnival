# Code of conduct

The carnival is a place to learn and build together. Everyone who takes part,
in this repository, in Discussions or at the workshop, follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, be patient with people who are learning, and assume good
intent. Harassment, personal attacks and unwelcome behavior of any kind aren't
OK here.

## Report a problem

Email **support@tessl.io**. Tell us what happened, where and when, and who was
involved. We keep reports private, and we'll get back to you.

At the workshop, you can also talk to any facilitator in person.

The Tessl team decides what happens next, following the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.
